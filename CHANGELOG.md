# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-10

Initial release.

- Normative specification derived from the article "The Agentic Data Development Lifecycle (ADDLC)" (Medium, November 2026): 67 numbered requirements across 11 groups, RFC 2119 keywords.
- Three conformance levels (Assisted, Spec-driven, Governed autonomy) plus the MRD profile for master and reference data.
- Normative terminology.
- Self-assessment questionnaire mapped to requirement IDs.
- Templates: intent brief, four gate checklists, autonomy register, handover package, metrics.
- Figures: model, autonomy ceilings, maturity (PNG, SVG, Excalidraw sources).
